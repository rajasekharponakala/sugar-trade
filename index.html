The Roots of Capitalism Slavery & the Sugar Trade

Prabir Purkayastha · Foreword by Vijay Prashad

Capitalism did not emerge from the gentle unfolding of markets and machines — but from the brutal reorganisation of the world through conquest, enslavement, and extraction. Trace sugar, enslaved labour, and the making of a global financial system on an interactive map and timeline.

§01 · The Conventional Narrative

Myth vs. fact

The “rise of the West” story airbrushes out looting, genocide, and slavery. Flip each claim to see what the historical record says.

Source: pp. 9–14. Scholars named in text: Eric Williams, Walter Rodney, C. L. R. James, Joseph Inikori; Marx; Claude Meillassoux; Sidney Mintz; Sven Beckert & Seth Rockman.

Foreword · Vijay Prashad

“Whose history is being told?”

Prabir Purkayastha wrote much of this study during 225-day incarceration in Rohini Central Jail, Delhi, on scraps of paper. Term note (p.7): the study uses “enslaved”, not “slave” — “Language is not neutral; it either reinforces or resists power.”
